# History

## Built by the engineer who ran document intake at national scale, then worked inside a firm's backlog until it moved.

Alpha Tensor is built by Brandon Moody, a software engineer working since 2008.

### The VA pipeline

At Aptive Resources, Brandon owned the data contract on the VA's veteran claims intake pipeline. The vendor supplied the extraction software. His team built everything around it.

His data structures had to match the vendor's configuration field for field, or nothing downstream worked. The pipeline took in essentially all veteran claims intake: FOIA responses, DD 214s, and death certificates in every permutation ever issued, many of them handwritten decades ago.

The VA and its vendor reported 96% accuracy and about $45M a year in costs reallocated. Those are the program's reported figures, and we attribute them to the program.

The lesson from that work shapes this company. Template-based extraction decays. Layouts drift, confidence drops, and the human review queue quietly grows until the queue is the product. Alpha Tensor reads a form's own structure instead, and keeps the review that remains standardized and measurable.

### Inside an immigration firm

Alpha Tensor's first deployment was inside a working immigration practice. Not a pilot on sample files: live cases, the firm's own rules for which packet a case needs, and the firm's attorneys reviewing what the system prepared.

That engagement set the pattern for the company. Do the work inside a real firm's rules, prove it on real cases, and let the firm's attorneys keep the judgment.

### Where it goes from here

Immigration is the first application, not the only one. The same approach applies to regulated paperwork beyond it: determine what a case needs, prepare it from the documents, and keep a qualified human in review.

The next step is wider. Proven inside firms first, this work will be offered to families directly, so people without a firm can still get a packet prepared and reviewed.
