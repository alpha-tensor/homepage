### One fact, every format the form asks for

Forms do not ask for the same fact the same way twice. One wants a name in full, the next wants a middle initial, the next wants first and last only. One takes an A-number as a single field, the next wants it letter by letter in separate boxes. One wants a full address on one line, the next wants it split into street, city, state, and ZIP.

Our system holds each fact once and renders it the way each form requires. Nobody retypes a variation by hand, and nobody has to catch the mismatch later. That polish is not a detail. It is the difference between a packet that looks filled in and a packet that is right.
