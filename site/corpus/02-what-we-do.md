# What we do

## We determine the packet, then we prepare it.

Most document work starts in the wrong place. Staff open a form and start typing. The harder question comes first: which packet does this case actually need?

Alpha Tensor answers that question first, then does the preparation work that follows from it.

### 1. Determination

We work out which process and which packet fit the case. We do this under rules the firm sets and approves. The firm decides how it practices. We apply its rules consistently, case after case and location after location, and we show our reasoning so staff can check it.

Determination is not a guess, and it is not a generic checklist. It is the firm's own case selection, applied before any form is touched.

### 2. Preparation

Once the packet is determined, we prepare it from the client's documents and answers.

The same fact is entered once and used everywhere it belongs, rendered the way each form asks for it: the same name in full on one form, initialed on the next, first and last on a third, without anyone retyping a variation by hand. Documents are read, facts are placed into the forms the packet requires, and evidence is organized for review. Staff stop retyping and start checking.

What we produce is a finished packet, ready for attorney review. Not a template. Not homework handed back to the firm.

### 3. Review stays with the attorney

We prepare work. We do not practise law.

Every packet goes to the firm's attorney for review before anything goes out. Legal judgment, filing decisions, and representation stay with the firm, where they belong.

### What this replaces

Hours of manual preparation: copying facts between forms, assembling packets by hand, and checking the same details repeatedly.

It does not replace the firm's judgment, its client relationships, or its attorneys.

### What we do not do

We do not file on the firm's behalf, and we do not promise outcomes. We also do not collect documents from clients for the firm. That work still takes time, and it stays with the firm. Our work starts with the documents and answers the firm has.
