# Solutions

## Preparation capacity for immigration firms, without another hire.

When preparation is manual, a firm cannot take on more cases without hiring more people to do the same work. Alpha Tensor breaks that link. The firm keeps its clients and its attorneys. We do the preparation work in between.

### For firms carrying a backlog

A backlog is rarely a demand problem. The clients are there. The hours are not. Cases sit because preparation is manual and the staff are already full.

Alpha Tensor was built inside exactly that situation: a working practice whose cases were waiting on preparation hours. Determination and preparation ran under the firm's rules, with its attorneys reviewing what came out.

The point is not speed for its own sake. It is cases moving again, with attorney review still in place.

### For firms that want to grow without growing headcount

Every case your team prepares by hand sets a ceiling on the next case you can accept. When determination and preparation are handled for you, staff time goes to clients and to review, not to retyping.

And this is not another tool for your staff to learn and feed. A tool hands the preparation back to you in a different view. We take the preparation itself.

You take on the cases in front of you. We handle the preparation behind them.

### For the firm's clients, later directly

Today, families reach this work through their firm. That will change.

The same determination and preparation engine will be offered to families directly: people who need to know which process fits their case, need the packet prepared, and need an attorney to review it, but who have no firm representing them today.

We are building toward that deliberately. Firms prove the work. Families are the larger need.

### An honest boundary

Collecting documents from clients still takes time, and we do not fix that. If documents never arrive, no system can prepare the packet. Our work begins once the firm has the documents and answers.

We would rather state that plainly than sell you a fix we do not have.
