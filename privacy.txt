Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. Who we are

Alpha Tensor LLC ("Alpha Tensor", "we", "us") operates this website, alphatensor.com, and provides document preparation services to law firms and, through our platform, to the clients they serve. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our consultation process, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Work we perform for a client under a signed engagement is governed by that engagement's terms, including its confidentiality provisions. This policy covers the website and pre-engagement communications. Where an engagement agreement says something different, the agreement controls for that work.

2. Information we collect

    Contact information: your name, email address, phone number, and firm or company name, when you book a consultation, email us, or otherwise contact us.
    Consultation information: the details you choose to share about your practice, your cases, and your document workflows, so we can assess whether we can help.
    Technical information: when you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically records standard log data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, and may set strictly necessary cookies for security and load balancing. This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
    We do not ask for client documents or other confidential case materials before an engagement is in place, and you should not send them to us before we have agreed on a secure transfer method.

3. How we use information

    To respond to your inquiry, and to schedule and conduct consultations.
    To provide, operate, and improve our services and this website.
    To communicate with you about your inquiry or engagement.
    To meet legal, accounting, and security obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Client documents and model training

Documents and data provided to us under an engagement are used only to perform that engagement. We do not use one client's documents to train models for other clients, and we do not disclose engagement materials except as the engagement requires and the client directs.

5. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the business:

    Service providers: companies that host this website and our email, provide scheduling (our booking page is operated by Cal.com), and provide the infrastructure our services run on. They process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
    Legal and safety: when required by law or court order, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
    Business transfers: in connection with a financing, merger, or sale of the company, subject to confidentiality.

6. Retention

We keep contact and consultation information for as long as needed to respond to you, maintain our business records, and meet legal obligations. Engagement materials are retained or deleted as the engagement agreement provides.

7. Security

We use administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information, including access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of non-essential communications at any time, by writing to support@alphatensor.com. We respond to requests as required by applicable law. Our services are directed to law firms and to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The current version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: support@alphatensor.com. Alpha Tensor LLC, Georgia, USA.
