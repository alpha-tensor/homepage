# Alpha Tensor Landing Page Redesign

## Purpose

This document is the implementation brief and source of truth for the Alpha Tensor landing page redesign.

The goal is to reposition Alpha Tensor around business capacity for immigration law firms, not around AI infrastructure, OCR, document processing, or generic legal technology.

The landing page should make a firm owner understand four things within roughly five seconds.

1. What Alpha Tensor does.
2. Why the firm should care.
3. Why the claim is credible.
4. What the visitor should do next.

The primary positioning is.

> Handle more immigration cases without adding staff.

Alpha Tensor should be presented as a system that removes repetitive operational work around immigration cases while fitting alongside the firm's existing systems and workflows.

---

# 1. Core Positioning

## Primary business outcome

Alpha Tensor helps immigration firms increase case capacity without increasing headcount.

This should be the dominant message across the page.

Do not lead with.

- AI.
- OCR.
- Document intelligence.
- Infrastructure.
- Automation platform.
- Legal operations software.
- Case management replacement.
- Technical architecture.

Those concepts may appear later as supporting evidence, but they are not the initial reason a buyer should care.

## Primary headline

> Handle more immigration cases without adding staff.

This is the current preferred hero headline.

It is strong because it connects directly to the economics of a law firm.

The visitor should immediately understand that Alpha Tensor is intended to improve capacity and operating leverage.

## Hero supporting copy

Preferred version.

> Alpha Tensor removes repetitive case work, from document sorting and data capture to routine review, so your team can spend more time moving cases forward.

Alternative shorter version.

> Alpha Tensor removes the repetitive work behind immigration cases, including document sorting, data capture, and routine review.

## Primary proof point

> Used across more than 1,100 immigration cases.

This proof should appear in or directly beneath the hero.

Do not bury this deep in the page.

## Primary CTA

Preferred.

> See Alpha Tensor in Action

Alternative.

> Book a Demo

The first option is preferred because it is lower pressure and directly connected to the product.

## Secondary CTA

Preferred.

> See the Workflow

Alternative.

> See It on Your Workflow

Do not use.

> Schedule Operations Review

This sounds like an audit, consulting engagement, or additional work for the visitor.

# 2. Positioning Rules

## What Alpha Tensor is

Alpha Tensor is an operational layer that removes repetitive case processing work.

The product currently supports or is being designed around areas including.

- Document intake.
- Document classification.
- Document organization.
- Structured data extraction.
- Data normalization.
- Routine case review.
- Evidence review.
- Case state tracking.
- Task and workflow management.
- Form filling.
- Document generation.
- Auditability.
- Existing file and workflow integration.

## What Alpha Tensor is not

The landing page should not position Alpha Tensor as.

- A replacement case management system.
- A generic legal AI assistant.
- A chatbot.
- An OCR vendor.
- A document storage vendor.
- A new proprietary document silo.
- A spreadsheet replacement product as the main value proposition.
- A full autonomous attorney.
- A system that performs unsupervised legal judgment.

## Core objection response

The strongest objection handling language is.

> We do not replace your case management system. We remove the manual work around it.

This should appear prominently on the page.

It addresses a likely buyer concern before the buyer has to ask.

A supporting sentence can be.

> Keep the systems your team already knows. Alpha Tensor handles the repetitive processing between incoming documents and usable case information.

Problem.

Problem.

Direction.

Use the phrase immigration firms or immigration cases in the hero.

## Dense hero UI

Problem.

Direction.

Show a curated product slice with one obvious outcome.

## Negative positioning

Problem.

Phrases such as.

> Not another SaaS dashboard.

Tell the visitor what Alpha Tensor is not without explaining why they should care.

Direction.

Replace negative positioning with positive business value.

## Mixed domain examples

Problem.

Direction.

## Weak CTA

Problem.

Direction.

# 23. Claims and Legal Safety

The marketing site must distinguish between operational review and legal judgment.

## Safe language

- Flag missing information.
- Surface inconsistencies.
- Identify uncertain fields.
- Prepare information for attorney review.
- Help staff find what needs attention.

## Avoid without strong legal and product support

- Approves a filing.
- Guarantees filing readiness.
- Performs legal review.
- Replaces attorney review.
- Ensures compliance.

Until then prefer.

- Ready for Review.
- Document Set Complete.
- Needs Attorney Review.
- Missing Evidence.

# 24. Proof Discipline

Do not invent evidence.

Every quantitative claim must have a source.

Current usable proof.

> More than 1,100 immigration cases processed using Alpha Tensor.

Before publishing additional metrics, document.

- Exact definition.
- Dataset.
- Time range.
- Measurement method.
- Whether metric is average, median, maximum, or anecdotal.
- Number of cases measured.

The landing page should not claim.

> From one hour to five minutes.

Until this has been measured across a defensible sample and the exact workflow is named.

When validated, use specific wording.

Example.

> In tested document intake workflows, median staff processing time fell from X to Y.

Specific metrics are more credible than broad claims.

# 38. Design Review Checklist

Reject a design if.

- It looks like a generic AI startup.
- It relies on gradients to create interest.
- Product screenshots are unreadable.
- The page feels like developer tooling.
- The page looks like a traditional law firm website.
- The visual system overwhelms the message.
- Too many sections compete equally.
- The homepage explains architecture before business value.
- The visitor must understand AI to understand the product.

Approve a direction when.

- The business outcome dominates.
- The product looks real.
- The page feels trustworthy.
- The product appears sophisticated without appearing difficult.
- The visitor can understand the workflow quickly.
